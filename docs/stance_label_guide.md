# Climate Stance Label Guide

This project classifies Hindi and Bengali comments
about climate change into three stance categories.

## 1. Favour
Comments that agree with the statement that
climate change and global warming are serious concerns.

Example:
जलवायु परिवर्तन एक गंभीर समस्या है

## 2. Against
Comments that disagree with or deny the seriousness
of climate change.

Example:
ग्लोबल वार्मिंग सब झूठ है

## 3. None
Comments that express no clear opinion or are
unrelated to the climate change statement.

Example:
कल दिल्ली में बारिश होगी

## Important Considerations

- Sarcasm can change the intended stance.
- Negation must be interpreted carefully.
- Romanized Hindi and Bengali should also be considered.
- Code-mixed comments may contain both English
  and Indic language words.

The classification task focuses on stance detection,
not sentiment analysis.
