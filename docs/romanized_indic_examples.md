Romanized Hindi and Bengali Examples

Introduction

Romanized text refers to Hindi and Bengali languages written using English alphabets.

Social media users frequently use Romanized text, informal spellings, and mixed-language expressions.

Handling such variations is an important challenge in Indic Climate Stance Detection.

1. Favour

Comments supporting the seriousness of climate change.

Romanized Hindi

* Jalvayu parivartan ek gambhir samasya hai.
* Global warming hamare bhavishya ke liye khatra hai.
* Humein environment ki raksha karni chahiye.

Romanized Bengali

* Jolbayu poriborton ekta gurutoro shomossha.
* Global warming amader bhobishoter jonno bipod.
* Amader poribesh rokkha kora uchit.

2. Against

Comments denying or disagreeing with climate change concerns.

Romanized Hindi

* Global warming sirf ek jhooth hai.
* Climate change koi serious problem nahi hai.
* Yeh sab bas afwah hai.

Romanized Bengali

* Global warming shudhu ekta mithya.
* Jolbayu poriborton kono gurutoro shomossha noy.
* Ei shob shudhu gujob.

3. None

Neutral or unrelated comments.

Romanized Hindi

* Kal Delhi mein baarish hogi.
* Aaj mausam bahut accha hai.

Romanized Bengali

* Kal Kolkata te brishti hobe.
* Aaj abohawa khub shundor.

Common Challenges

1. Multiple spellings for the same word.
2. Informal abbreviations.
3. English and Indic language mixing.
4. Missing vowels and spelling variations.
5. Context-dependent meaning.

Conclusion

Romanized text handling is important for building robust multilingual stance detection systems.

These examples are illustrative and are not additional training or evaluation data.
